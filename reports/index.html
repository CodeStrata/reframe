Reframe: example reports

What Reframe makes from mainframe source, on AWS's open-source CardDemo application. Nothing to install: click through the business reviews, the reconciliation, the prototype and the package. Every page is model output, for people to review, and cleaned of the identifiers of the account that ran it.

CardDemo

AWS's open-source credit-card application: 44 COBOL programs, 21 screens. Both assessments (AWS Transform and Claude), compared and reconciled against the code, then the design: legacy and modern screens, journeys, a clickable prototype, final specifications and the AI-DLC package. 1,147 pages.
