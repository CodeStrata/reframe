# Product: genapp, modernized

The modernized application, designed by Reframe from the legacy source's reconciled requirements and rules. Signed-off functions are final; the rest are draft.

## Functions

- **Application Deployment Lifecycle Management: Application Deployment and Policy Lifecycle Management (ADPLM)**, draft: 21 capabilities
- **Customer Record Management: Customer Record Creation and Maintenance (CRCM)**, draft: 7 capabilities
- **Programs not in a business function: Unassigned-Programs (UP)**, draft: 13 capabilities
- **Unassigned programs: Unassigned programs (UP2)**, draft: 5 capabilities
- **Unclassified: Diagnostic and Error Message Logging (DEML)**, draft: 2 capabilities
- **Unclassified: Transaction Statistics and Monitoring (TSM)**, draft: 4 capabilities
- **Web Service Interface Generation: Web Service Interface Generation (WSIG)**, draft: 5 capabilities

## Journeys

- J01 Add a new customer: Create a new customer record and get the customer number the system assigns to it.
- J02 Update a customer's details: Find an existing customer and change their details so that the record stays current.
- J03 Manage a motor policy: Look up, add, change or delete a customer's motor policy so that the motor cover on record is correct.
- J04 Manage a life (endowment) policy: Look up, add, change or delete a customer's endowment policy so that their life cover is recorded correctly.
- J05 Manage a house policy: Add, change or delete a customer's house policy so that their property cover is recorded correctly.
- J06 Find, add or delete a commercial property policy: Find commercial property policies by customer, policy number or postcode, and add or delete them so that commercial cover, perils and premiums are recorded correctly.
